# Featured LLM social simulations

ORDO retains published interaction environments and replaces their choice engine with Jev. The current collection covers convention formation and committed minorities (Science Advances, Ashery et al.), majority coordination (Science Advances, De Marzo et al.), repeated games (Nature Human Behaviour, Akata et al.), and network polarization (Nature Communications, Piao et al.). Methods, configurations and execution scope are recorded in the technical appendix.

Convention formation reaches the criterion in 8/8 Jev worlds, versus 0/8 uniform-control worlds. The minority conditions yield final ordinary-member M shares of 1.4%, 96.6%, 98.2% and 99.0% for 0, 4, 5 and 8 committed members. Majority coordination reaches consensus in 16/20 worlds with mean final coordination 0.952. Repeated games contain 320 fresh Jev choices, with 81 cooperative choices. The polarization run completes 11 of 40 epochs for 1,000 members; the recorded data retain its actual horizon.

The organizational extension adds 1,000 members, 50 departments, reporting levels and publishing permissions. Twelve Jev worlds and 36 rule worlds produce 92,058 fresh Jev choices. Under formal guidance, ordinary-member M adoption is 6.3% with committed employees and 22.8% with committed managers. This is an extension of the convention mechanism, reported separately from source replications.

New cases should preserve population, horizon, actions, visible history and update order for the selected source condition, and record model substitution and text-generation components explicitly. All source protocols, receipts and saved-probability replays are linked from the appendix.
