# Nature Communications and Nature Computational Science landscape

Relevant source studies span mobility, organizational communication, collective foraging, evolutionary games and LLM-agent networks. The first group often uses explicit behavioral rules or empirical estimation. These are valuable ABM environments, but their adaptation does not establish reproduction of an existing LLM simulation.

Our featured-source criterion is an LLM behavioral engine with repeated interaction and endogenous feedback. Candidate references and protocol comparisons below document the screening process; the main appendix indexes the selected cases.

## Configuration and execution

| Source study | Source research question | Relation to ORDO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Emergence of polarization in networks of large language model agents](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-78228-y), 2026 | Thousands of LLM agents generate communication and update opinions and ties; the study examines polarization, homophily, interaction diversity and confirmation-bias interventions. | Original LLM social-simulation candidate with generated-content feedback. A hybrid can retain fresh LLM communication and assign closed stance/tie choices to Jev; report the component boundary. |
| [Disentangling interaction and bias effects in opinion dynamics of large language models](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-77340-3), 2026 | Two LLM agents debate 12 topics over five rounds, using six models to examine communication, prior attractors and measurement bias. | Multiround interaction source. Jev can provide stance decisions or measurements; a complete hybrid must identify its communication and choice modules. |
